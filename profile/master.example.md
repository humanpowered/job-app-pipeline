# Master accomplishment record

Copy this to `profile/master.md` and fill it in. `master_profile.json` is built
from it, so this file is the one to maintain.

**There is no page limit here, and that is the point.** A resume holds the
accomplishments that suited one application; this holds everything you have
done, and the tailoring step picks from it per posting. A role with three
accomplishments gives the model three to choose from. A role with fifteen gives
it room to find the ones this employer cares about.

You will not remember everything in one sitting. Add to it over months; that is
what it is for. If you have ever filled in a resume-development questionnaire
for a career coach, `python src/import_intake.py "that file.docx"` will write
most of this for you.

Then one block per accomplishment, as many as you can recall. Problem, actions
and results — not a finished resume bullet. The raw material is more useful than
a polished sentence, because a bullet written for one employer rarely fits the
next one, and the pipeline can compose the sentence itself.

Put a number in **Results** whenever you have one. Dollars, percent, counts,
time saved, people hired. The grounding lint will only let a number into a
document if it appears here first.

#### Paid search efficiency program

- **Problem:** Acquisition cost had risen 40% over two years while last-click
  reporting showed paid search as the top performer, so budget kept flowing to
  it.
- **Actions:** Built geo-holdout tests across the ten largest markets, then an
  MMM covering four channels to reconcile the holdout results with the
  attribution the team already trusted.
- **Results:** Cut acquisition cost 23% in two quarters with no revenue loss.

#### Built the measurement practice

- **Problem:** No one owned measurement; three teams reported different numbers
  for the same campaign.
- **Actions:** Hired four analysts, published one definition per metric, moved
  reporting onto a single warehouse model.
- **Results:** One source of truth adopted by marketing, finance and the board.
  Reporting time down from six days a month to half a day.
